Welcome to the Uganda Fan Portal

Uganda is proud to host official FIFA World Cup 2026 fan-zone activities across the country. As the Pearl of Africa, we welcome football fans from around the world to experience the beautiful game in our vibrant communities.

The Ministry of Sports and Tourism, in collaboration with FIFA, has established verified fan zones in major cities to ensure a safe, enjoyable, and authentic World Cup experience for all.

How to Register for Your Official Fan-Zone Pass

  1. Visit the Ministry's official portal and create an account
  2. Verify your identity using a valid government-issued ID
  3. Select your preferred fan-zone location from the list of venues
  4. Complete the online registration form with your details
  5. Receive your digital fan-zone pass via email and SMS

Permitted Items in Official Fan Zones

To ensure safety and enjoyment for all, fans are permitted to bring the following items into official fan zones:

  • 🇺🇬 Uganda national flags (max size 1.5m x 1m)
  • 📱 Mobile phones and portable chargers
  • 🧴 Sunscreen lotion (SPF 30+)
  • 🧢 Caps and hats for sun protection
  • 💧 Reusable water bottles (empty upon entry)
  • 🎒 Small backpacks or bags (max 30cm x 20cm)

Official Announcement

Verified Fan-Zone Locations Announced

Kampala, Uganda — August 15, 2026 — The Ministry of Sports and Tourism, in partnership with FIFA, has today confirmed the official fan-zone locations for the FIFA World Cup 2026. The venues, spread across four regions of Uganda, will host live screenings, cultural performances, and interactive football activities throughout the tournament.

"We are committed to providing an unforgettable World Cup experience for both local and international fans," said Minister of Sports, Hon. Peter Ogwang. "All fan zones will adhere to strict safety standards, and entry is free for registered fans with a valid fan-zone pass. We encourage all football enthusiasts to register early and avoid unofficial gatherings."

The full list of venues, including capacities and accessibility information, is now available on the Venues page. Fans are advised to rely only on information from this official portal to avoid fake ticketing scams.
